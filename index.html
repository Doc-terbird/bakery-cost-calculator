FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. The download is a macro-free .xlsx built with ordinary formulas that also open in Google Sheets. Excel and Sheets both work; you’ll need compatible software or a Google account for Sheets.

Is this legal, tax, or food-safety advice?

No. It is a costing aid only. It does not provide legal, tax, accounting, nutrition, allergen, or food-safety advice. Cottage-food rules vary by place — check your local requirements separately.

What’s the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit divided by cost. Margin is profit divided by selling price. A 50% markup on a $2 cost is $3; a 50% margin on a $2 cost needs a $4 price. The workbook shows both so you don’t mix them up.

Will scaling a recipe guarantee the same bake?

No. Mathematical scaling adjusts quantities. It does not validate bake time, pan geometry, leavening, or results. Always test.
